## Content & SEO Takeaways for PixPassport

1. **Keyword & Intent Alignment:** Commercial buyers search across four distinct verticals: photo studios/print labs, pharmacies/shipping/postal counters, visa/travel agencies, and HR/university credentialing teams. Covering all four explicitly captures long-tail B2B intent without creating thin doorway pages.
2. **Interactive Utility Advantage:** None of the top-ranking competitors provide an interactive **Profit & Print Sheet Yield Calculator** showing exact photos-per-sheet on 4×6, 5×7, 6×8, A4, and Letter paper plus monthly gross profit and time saved vs manual Photoshop cropping.
3. **Honest Differentiation:** Highlight PixPassport's real strengths verifiable in the product today:
   - Zero-install cross-platform browser workspace (macOS, Windows, iPad, Chromebook, Linux)
   - 81 official document specifications covering 70 countries
   - 30+ automated ICAO Doc 9303 / national biometric compliance checks (`Pass/Fail`) before printing or uploading
   - Dual output: exact digital portal specs (US 600×600 px, UK, Schengen 413×531 px, India) + multi-paper 300 DPI print sheets (3.5×5, 4×6, 5×7, 6×8, 8×10, A6–A3, Letter, Legal, Tabloid, custom mm in PDF/JPG/PNG with crop lines)
   - 24-hour auto-deletion privacy posture with TLS 1.3 & AES-256 transient encryption (`/data-security`), plus optional human expert review (`/expert-edit`) and custom volume inquiries (`/contact`).
