Permanent resident card applications and renewals use the same 50 × 70 mm format as a Canadian passport photo. The details are easy to get wrong, so here is what to prepare.

## PR Card Photo Specifications

- **Size:** 50 mm wide × 70 mm high (2 × 2¾ inches)
- **Face height:** chin to crown 31–36 mm
- **Copies:** two identical photos
- **Background:** plain white or light-coloured
- **Expression:** neutral, eyes open, mouth closed
- **Age:** taken within the last 6 months

## Photographer Information on the Back

One of the two photos must carry the photographer's information on the back. Not every application also needs a guarantor signature, so read your application form before the photo session and tell the photographer exactly what to write.

## Common Reasons PR Card Photos Are Returned

- Wrong dimensions, such as the 35 × 45 mm visa size
- Face too large or too small in the frame
- Shadows, patterns or a coloured background
- Glare on glasses, or a smile or open mouth
- Photos edited or filtered after they were taken
