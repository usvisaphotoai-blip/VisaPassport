## UK Visas & Immigration (UKVI) Photo Guidelines

Whether you are applying for a UK Tourist Visa, Student Visa, Skilled Worker Visa, or Biometric Residence Permit (BRP), your photograph must comply with UKVI guidelines.

### Technical Photo Criteria for UK Visas

- **Format:** 35mm x 45mm color photo
- **Photo Quality:** Printed on crisp photo paper or saved as high-resolution JPEG (minimum 600 DPI)
- **Recency:** Must have been taken within the past 1 month
- **Lighting:** Uniform lighting without red-eye, shadows, or harsh reflections
