## 1. UK Driving License Photo Size at a Glance

The DVLA does not publish a separate size for driving licences in Great Britain. It asks for a passport-style photo, so the UK passport photo size applies. This is the DVLA photo size you need:

| Measurement                 | Value                                                |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Print size (mm)             | 35 x 45 mm                                           |
| Print size (cm)             | 3.5 x 4.5 cm                                         |
| Print size (inches)         | 1.38 x 1.77 in                                       |
| Aspect ratio                | 7:9 (width to height)                                |
| Head height (chin to crown) | 29-34 mm (1.14-1.34 in)                              |
| Print-ready pixels          | 413 x 531 px at 300 dpi                              |
| Online upload minimum       | 600 x 750 px                                         |
| File format and size        | JPEG, between 50 KB and 10 MB                        |
| Colour                      | Colour only                                          |
| Background                  | Plain light-coloured (cream or light grey is safest) |

Looking for the driving licence photo size in pixels? A print-ready file is 413 x 531 px at 300 dpi, and online uploads need at least 600 x 750 px.

Take your photo recently. The passport photo rules ask for a photo taken in the last month, and your photo stays on your photocard for up to 10 years, so a true likeness matters.

## 2. DVLA Photo Requirements: What Your Driving Licence Photo Must Show

The DVLA photo requirements follow the standard UK passport photo rules on gov.uk. A valid driving licence photo must:

- be clear, in focus, in colour and unaltered by computer software.
- show your full head and upper shoulders, facing forwards and looking straight at the camera.
- show a plain expression with your mouth closed and your eyes open and visible.
- have no hair in front of your eyes and nothing covering your face.
- be taken against a plain light-coloured background that contrasts with you.
- have no shadows on your face or behind you, no red-eye, and no other people or objects.
- have no head covering unless it is worn for religious or medical reasons.

Glasses and smiling are the two most common questions, so we answer both directly in the [FAQs below](#11-driving-licence-photo-faqs). For hair, makeup, jewellery and clothing, read our full [UK driving licence photo rules](/uk/driving-licence-photo-rules).

## 3. Provisional License Photo Rules

Provisional license photo rules are the same as for a full licence: a recent passport-style photo that meets the DVLA photo requirements above. What changes is the application process.

- **Age:** you can apply for your first provisional licence from 15 years and 9 months old.
- **Cost:** a first provisional licence costs £34 online or £43 by post.
- **Online:** apply on gov.uk and upload a digital photo. The service runs its own checks and tells you if the photo is rejected.
- **By post:** use a D1 form from a Post Office that handles DVLA applications and attach a printed passport-style photo. Follow the D1 instructions on whether your photo needs to be countersigned.
- **After your test:** your first full licence is free by post if you keep your provisional photo, or £17 if you send a different one.

## 6. Driving Licence Photo Printing for a Paper D1 Form

For a postal application, attach **one recent printed photo** at 35 x 45 mm to your D1 form. Use the printable sheet from our tool for your driving licence photo print, then:

- Print on plain white photographic paper with no border and to a professional standard. Plain paper from a home inkjet is not suitable.
- Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page") so the photo measures exactly 35 x 45 mm. It must not be a cut-down version of a larger picture.
- Cut along the edges. Do not staple the photo to the form unless the D1 instructions say so.
- Keep it unmarked on both sides, unless it needs to be countersigned.

Read the instructions on your form too, because forms are updated from time to time. For what to write on the back and who can sign, see the [provisional licence photo guide](/uk/provisional-driving-licence-photo-guide).

## 7. Photo Driving Licence Renewal: When You May Need a New Photo

You must renew a photocard driving licence every 10 years, and you will get a reminder before it ends. Renewing is also how you change your photo: you get a new licence, and you can use your passport photo or upload a new one.

### 7.1 Updating Driving Licence Photo Details Online or by Post

You can change your photo at any time, not only at the 10-year point. Consider a new one if:

- your 10-year renewal is due.
- you look very different from your current photo.
- you are moving from a provisional to a full licence and want a different photo (£17 by post).

Changing the photo costs £14 online or £17 by post. You cannot renew online if your name or title has changed since you got your licence, or if you hold a 5-year bus or lorry licence. In those cases, apply by post. This applies in Great Britain; Northern Ireland uses a separate DVA service.

## 8. Background, Lighting and Head Position

These settings affect whether your photo is accepted:

- **Background:** plain and light-coloured, with no patterns or objects. Cream or light grey works best because your head and shoulders must stand out clearly.
- **Lighting:** even light across your face, with no shadows on your face or behind you.
- **Position:** face the camera directly, with no tilt, and keep your head and shoulders in frame.
- **Head size:** 29-34 mm from chin to crown inside the 35 x 45 mm frame.
- **Eyes:** free of glare and red-eye, with no hair across them.

## 9. Why UK Driving Licence Photos Get Rejected (Size and Format Errors)

Most technical rejections come from size and file problems:

1. **Head too large or too small**, outside the 29-34 mm range.
2. **Wrong crop or ratio**, not 7:9 (35 x 45 mm).
3. **Low resolution**, below 600 x 750 pixels.
4. **File too small or too large**, outside 50 KB to 10 MB.
5. **Weak contrast**, such as a white or patterned background.
6. **Shadows** across your face or the background.
7. **Filters or retouching**, including beauty modes on your phone.

### 9.1 How to Fix a Rejected Photo

- Retake the photo against a plain, light wall if the background or lighting was the issue.
- Export the file at 600 x 750 pixels or larger, as a JPEG.
- Check the file size is between 50 KB and 10 MB.

## 11. Driving Licence Photo FAQs

### 11.1 What size photo do I need for a UK driving licence?

Your photo must be 35 mm wide by 45 mm tall (3.5 x 4.5 cm), the same size as a UK passport photo, with a head height of 29-34 mm from chin to crown.

### 11.2 What is the UK driving licence photo size in inches and pixels?

35 x 45 mm is 1.38 x 1.77 inches, a 7:9 ratio. A print-ready file is 413 x 531 pixels at 300 dpi. For online upload, gov.uk asks for a colour image at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall, between 50 KB and 10 MB.

### 11.3 How do I upload a driving licence photo online?

Make your photo with the tool above, download the JPEG, then choose the photo upload option in your gov.uk driving licence application and select the file. The service runs its own checks and tells you if the photo is not accepted.

### 11.4 Can I wear glasses in my driving licence photo?

Only if you have to. The DVLA follows the passport photo rules: do not wear glasses unless you must. If you do, they cannot be sunglasses or tinted, and the frames, glare, reflections or shadows must not cover your eyes.

### 11.5 Can I smile in my driving licence photo?

No. You need a plain expression with your mouth closed and your eyes open and visible. A relaxed, neutral face is the safest choice.

### 11.6 What are the photo requirements for a provisional licence?

They are the same as for a full licence: a recent, passport-style colour photo, 35 x 45 mm if printed, taken against a plain light-coloured background. You can apply online or by post from 15 years and 9 months old. A first provisional licence costs £34 online or £43 by post.

### 11.7 Can I use my passport photo for my driving licence?

When you apply or renew online, gov.uk says you can use your passport photo or upload a new one. For a postal D1 application, include a recent printed passport-type photo.

### 11.8 How do I change the photo on my driving licence?

Renew your licence to get a new photo. Changing the photo costs £14 online or £17 by post, and you get a new licence. You can do it at any time, and photocard licences must be renewed every 10 years.
