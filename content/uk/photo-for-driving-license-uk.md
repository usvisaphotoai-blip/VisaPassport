## 1. UK Driving Licence Photo Size at a Glance

The DVLA does not publish a separate size for driving licences in Great Britain. It asks for a passport-style photo, so the UK passport photo size applies.

| Measurement                 | Value                         |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Print size (mm)             | 35 x 45 mm                    |
| Print size (cm)             | 3.5 x 4.5 cm                  |
| Print size (inches)         | 1.38 x 1.77 in                |
| Aspect ratio                | 7:9 (width to height)         |
| Head height (chin to crown) | 29-34 mm (1.14-1.34 in)       |
| Print-ready pixels          | 413 x 531 px at 300 dpi       |
| Online upload minimum       | 600 x 750 px                  |
| File format and size        | JPEG, between 50 KB and 10 MB |
| Colour                      | Colour only                   |

Take your photo recently, ideally within the last month. Your photo appears on your photocard for up to 10 years, so a true likeness matters.

## 4. How to Print Your Photo for a Paper D1 Form

For a postal application, attach **one recent printed photo** at 35 x 45 mm to your D1 form.

- Print on proper photo paper, because plain paper from a home inkjet is not suitable.
- Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page") so the photo stays exactly 35 x 45 mm.
- Cut along the edges and do not staple the photo to the form.
- Do not write on the front of the photo.

Read the instructions on your form too, because forms are updated from time to time. For what to write on the back and who can sign, see the [provisional licence photo guide](/uk/provisional-driving-licence-photo-guide).

## 5. Background, Lighting and Head Position

These settings affect whether your photo is accepted:

- **Background:** plain light grey or cream, with no patterns or objects. Avoid white, a very common cause of rejection.
- **Lighting:** even light across your face, with no shadows on your face or behind you.
- **Position:** face the camera directly, with no tilt, and keep your head and shoulders in frame.
- **Head size:** 29-34 mm from chin to crown inside the 35 x 45 mm frame.
- **Eyes:** free of glare and red-eye, with no hair across them.

Our tool applies the correct background and head size automatically, so you do not need a studio backdrop at home.

### 5.1 Glasses, Smiling and Other Appearance Rules

The short version: take your glasses off if you can, keep a neutral expression with your mouth closed, and avoid hats. Religious head coverings are fine when your full face stays visible.

## 6. Why UK Driving Licence Photos Get Rejected (Size and Format Errors)

Most technical rejections come from size and file problems:

1. **Head too large or too small**, outside the 29-34 mm range.
2. **Wrong crop or ratio**, not 7:9 (35 x 45 mm).
3. **Low resolution**, below 600 x 750 pixels.
4. **File too small or too large**, outside 50 KB to 10 MB.
5. **White background** instead of light grey or cream.
6. **Shadows** across your face or the background.
7. **Filters or retouching**, including beauty modes on your phone.

### How to Fix a Rejected Photo

- Re-upload your original photo to the tool above so it is cropped and sized correctly.
- Retake the photo against a plain, light wall if the background or lighting was the issue.
- Export the file at 600 x 750 pixels or larger, as a JPEG.
- Check the file size is between 50 KB and 10 MB.

## 8. UK Driving Licence Photo Size FAQs

### 8.1 What size photo do I need for a UK driving licence?

Your photo must be 35 mm wide by 45 mm tall (3.5 x 4.5 cm), the same as a UK passport photo. Your head should measure 29-34 mm from chin to crown.

### 8.2 What is the UK driving licence photo size in inches and pixels?

35 x 45 mm is 1.38 x 1.77 inches, a 7:9 ratio. A print-ready file is 413 x 531 pixels at 300 dpi. For online upload, use a JPEG of at least 600 x 750 pixels, between 50 KB and 10 MB.

### 8.3 How do I upload a driving licence photo online?

Make your photo with the tool above, download the JPEG, then choose the photo upload option in your gov.uk driving licence application and select the file. The service runs its own checks and tells you if the photo is not accepted.
