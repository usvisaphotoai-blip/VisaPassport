## What are the EAD photo requirements?

Form I-765 asks for **two identical color photos** that are **{{spec.uscis-photo.size}}** and taken recently. Your head must measure **{{spec.uscis-photo.headIn}}** from the top of your hair to the bottom of your chin.

The instructions add a few more rules. The background must be white to off-white. The prints must be on thin, glossy paper, and USCIS wants them unmounted and unretouched.

## Do all EAD categories need photos?

Yes. The I-765 instructions list the photos under **Required Documentation**. That section opens with “You must file all applications with the documents required below,” so it covers every category, from OPT and (c)(9) to asylum (c)(8) and TPS.

The same goes for renewals and replacements. You send two new prints each time you file.

## How does USCIS get the photo for your EAD card?

Since **December 12, 2025**, the photo on your EAD comes from a USCIS biometrics appointment, not from the prints you mail. Policy Alert PA-2025-29 set three rules:

1. USCIS **will not use or reuse self-submitted photos** on the cards it issues.
2. USCIS can **reuse a biometrics photo** only if it took that photo no more than **3 years** before you file.
3. USCIS **may still ask for a new photo** instead of reusing an older one.

Here is what that means in practice:

| Your situation | What to expect |
|---|---|
| You had a USCIS biometrics appointment less than 3 years before you file | USCIS may reuse that photo for your card. |
| Your last biometrics photo is older than 3 years, or you never had one | Expect a biometrics appointment notice after you file. |
| You file I-765 together with Form I-485 | Form I-485 always needs a new photo, so you will attend biometrics. |

The policy did not change the I-765 instructions. They still ask for two photos, so include them with your form.

## Which version of Form I-765 should you use?

Use the edition the USCIS form page lists as accepted. On **November 10, 2026**, that was the **08/21/25 edition**. A court order from September 14, 2026 keeps USCIS on that version, so it turns away the newer 09/15/26 edition for now.

USCIS can reject a form if its pages come from different editions. Check the edition date at the bottom of every page before you mail it.

## How do you take an EAD photo yourself?

You can take an EAD photo with a phone and print it at a photo lab.

1. **Pick a plain wall in white or off-white** and face a window, so daylight falls evenly on your face.
2. **Have someone else take the picture** at your eye level, from arm’s length or farther.
3. **Look straight at the camera** with a neutral face and both eyes open.
4. **Upload the photo to the tool above.** It crops your photo to {{spec.uscis-photo.sizeIn}} and checks your head size.
5. **Print two copies** on glossy photo paper from the same file.
6. **Label the back lightly** in pencil or felt pen: your name, plus your A-Number once USCIS has given you one.

USCIS asks for photos that are not edited or digitally enhanced. Start with a clean, plain wall so the tool only needs to crop your photo, and never use filters or face-smoothing effects.

## Common EAD photo mistakes and how to fix them

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sending one photo | Send two identical prints with every I-765. |
| Reusing the prints from an old filing | Take new photos. The instructions ask for photos taken recently. |
| Head too small in the frame | Crop closer so your head is {{spec.uscis-photo.headIn}} from hair to chin. |
| Colored or busy background | Retake the photo with a bare white or off-white wall behind you. |
| Photo edited in a beauty app | Use the original camera file, with no filters. |
| Prints from different photo files | Print both copies from one file at the same time. |
| One set of photos for a combined I-485, I-765 and I-131 package | Prepare a separate set of photos for each form. |

## What about glasses and head coverings?

The I-765 instructions don’t mention glasses. We suggest you take them off, because lens glare can hide your eyes.

Your head must be bare unless your religion requires a head covering. If you wear one, keep your whole face visible from hairline to chin.
