## Overview: Canada Temporary Resident Visa (TRV / V-1) in 2026

The **Canada Visitor Visa (Tourist Visa Category V-1)** allows foreign nationals to enter Canada for vacation, visiting family and friends, or attending short non-work events. Issued by **Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)**, a Canadian tourist visa is typically granted as a **multiple-entry visa valid for up to 10 years** (or until your passport expires).

Each entry allows a stay of up to **6 months** (180 days). Submitting a thorough, well-indexed document dossier is the single most decisive factor in securing an approval on your first attempt.

## Step-by-Step Document Assembly Guide

### 1. Proof of Financial Sufficiency
IRCC visa officers evaluate whether you can sustain yourself and your accompanying family members without resorting to unauthorized employment or public funds in Canada.

- **Primary Bank Account (6 Months):** Provide continuous bank statements showing regular salary deposits or business income. Avoid sudden, massive deposits immediately before applying; if you received funds (e.g., matured fixed deposit or bonus), include supporting transfer receipts.
- **Estimated Funds Formula:**
  $$\text{Recommended Balance} = \text{Flights} + \text{Hotels} + (\text{Days in Canada} \times \$150\text{ CAD/day})$$
- **Supplementary Assets:** Tax returns (last 3 years), investment portfolios, property deeds, and provident fund statements reinforce your financial stability.

## Top 5 Reasons for Canada Visa Refusals & How to Prevent Them

1. **IRCC Section 179(b) - Home Country Ties:** The officer doubts you will return. Provide employment contracts, leave approval letters, business licenses, or property ownership documents.
2. **Unexplained Bank Deposits:** Large sudden inflows trigger suspicion of borrowed funds. Always provide documentation for unusual credits.
3. **Vague Travel Purpose:** Submitting a generic cover letter stating "Tourism" without day-by-day city itineraries, museum bookings, or hotel arrangements raises red flags.
4. **Discrepancies in Application Forms:** Ensure dates of previous travel in form **IMM 5257** match your passport entry/exit stamps exactly.
5. **Non-compliant Biometric Photo:** Ensure your photo is exactly 35x45mm with no glare or tilted head.

## Cover Letter (Statement of Purpose) Template Structure

A compelling cover letter should be addressed to **The Visa Officer, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)** and include:
- **Section 1: Applicant Profile & Objective:** Your current profession, company, monthly salary, and primary objective of visiting Canada.
- **Section 2: Travel Itinerary:** Precise dates of entry, planned cities (e.g., Toronto, Vancouver, Banff), and lodging addresses.
- **Section 3: Financial Source:** Summary of your funds allocated for the trip and how payments (hotels/flights) will be handled.
- **Section 4: Ties to Home Country:** Overview of your family dependents, permanent job duties, and assets guaranteeing prompt return.
