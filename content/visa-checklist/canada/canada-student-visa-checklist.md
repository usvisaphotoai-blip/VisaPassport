## Canada Student Visa & Study Permit Guide 2026

Obtaining a **Canadian Study Permit** is the legal gateway for international students to pursue higher education at a **Designated Learning Institution (DLI)** in Canada. Issued by **Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)**, a study permit allows you to live in Canada for the duration of your academic program plus an additional **90 days** to transition to a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) or depart the country.

## 1. Key IRCC Policy Updates for 2026

Before gathering your application files, ensure you understand the major regulatory updates implemented by IRCC:

### A. Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) Requirement
Unless enrolled in a graduate degree program (Master's or Doctoral), nearly all undergraduate and post-secondary diploma students must obtain a **Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL)** issued by the provincial government where your university or college is located. The PAL must be uploaded directly with your initial study permit dossier.

### B. Updated Financial Threshold ($20,635 CAD GIC)
The cost-of-living benchmark requires single applicants to demonstrate at least **$20,635 CAD** in liquid funds through a **Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC)** held at an approved Canadian bank (such as CIBC, Scotiabank, ICICI Bank Canada, or RBC).

### C. Off-Campus Work Authorization
Full-time students in eligible programs can work **up to 24 hours per week off-campus** without an independent work permit during academic semesters, providing practical income support while maintaining full-time academic focus.

## 3. Financial Dossier & Proof of Funds Breakdown

IRCC visa officers evaluate financial capacity with rigorous scrutiny. Insufficient funds or sudden unverified deposits are the primary reason for application refusals.

### Mandatory Financial Checklist:
1. **Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC):** Minimum $20,635 CAD deposited in an approved Canadian financial institution.
2. **First-Year Tuition Payment Confirmation:** Official receipt showing full payment of year-1 tuition to your Designated Learning Institution.
3. **6 Months Certified Bank Statements:** Maintained by you or your financial sponsors, demonstrating stable balances and verifiable sources of income.
4. **Income Tax Returns (ITR):** Last 3 consecutive financial years of tax assessments for all contributing sponsors.
5. **Affidavit of Financial Support:** Sworn declaration outlining the sponsor's relationship, net worth, and formal commitment to support your education.

---

## 4. Statement of Purpose (SOP) & Study Plan Blueprint

Your **Study Plan** is your personal voice before the visa officer. A strong SOP must clearly address the following four pillars:

* **Why this specific program and institution?** Explain why the curriculum at your chosen DLI aligns with your previous academic background and career goals.
* **Why study in Canada instead of your home country?** Highlight the advanced academic standards, research facilities, and practical industry exposure available in Canada.
* **How will this degree accelerate your career in your home country?** Provide specific job roles, prospective employers, and salary prospects in your home nation upon completion.
* **Proof of Strong Ties to Your Home Country:** Detail family obligations, economic assets, property, or ongoing business ties that guarantee your return upon graduation.

## 5. Biometric Photo Guidelines (35x45mm)

Your visa photograph must meet IRCC's exact biometric standards to avoid processing holds or rejection at the Visa Application Centre:

* **Dimensions:** 35 mm wide x 45 mm high.
* **Face Height:** The distance from the bottom of your chin to the top of your head must be between **31 mm and 36 mm** (70% to 80% of total height).
* **Background:** Solid pure white with zero shadows or patterns.
* **Expression:** Neutral facial expression, mouth closed, eyes clearly visible and looking directly at the camera.
* **Eyeglasses:** Strictly prohibited under IRCC regulations.
