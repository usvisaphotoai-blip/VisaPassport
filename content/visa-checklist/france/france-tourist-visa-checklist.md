## France tourist visa from India: the essentials

A France tourist visa is a **short-stay Schengen visa (type C)**. It lets you stay up to **90 days in any 180-day period** and travel across all Schengen countries. Apply through France if France is your main destination, or your first entry point when no single country is the main one.

The exact documents depend on your purpose of travel, your job and who is paying. Use the interactive checklist above, then compare it against the official checklist for your category on France-Visas.

### Passport requirements

Your passport must:

- Have been issued within the last **10 years**
- Be valid for at least **3 months** after your planned departure from the Schengen Area
- Have at least **2 blank pages**
- Be in good condition

### How to apply from India

1. **Fill in the form** on [France-Visas](https://france-visas.gouv.fr) and print the signed form and receipt.
2. **Book an appointment** with [VFS Global India](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/ind/en/fra).
3. **Attend the appointment** with your appointment letter, documents and fees. Fingerprints and a photo are taken there.
4. **Track your application** and collect your passport from VFS once a decision is made.

### Photo requirements

France follows the ICAO standard: **35 x 45 mm**, colour, plain light background, face 32–36 mm from chin to crown, neutral expression, taken within the last 6 months.
