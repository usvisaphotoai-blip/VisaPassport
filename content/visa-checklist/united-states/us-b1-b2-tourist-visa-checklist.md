## US B1/B2 Visa Document Guide 2026

The **B1/B2 Nonimmigrant Visa** is the primary entry document for individuals traveling to the United States for temporary business (**B-1**) or tourism, vacation, and medical treatment (**B-2**).

In 2026, most consular posts issue **10-year multiple-entry visas**, allowing visits up to **6 months per entry**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        US VISA 2026 ESSENTIALS                         │
├──────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Application Form     │ DS-160 (Online Nonimmigrant Visa Application)   │
│ Government Fee       │ $185 USD (MRV Fee)                              │
│ Photo Specification  │ 2 × 2 Inches (51 × 51 mm / 600 × 600 px)        │
│ Biometric Center     │ VAC / OFC (Fingerprints + Digital Photo)        │
│ Final Decision       │ Given verbally during Consular Interview        │
└──────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### US 2x2 Inch Photo Compliance
The US Department of State uses automated facial biometric readers:
- **Head Size:** Chin to top of head must be between **1 inch and 1 3/8 inches** (50% to 69% of image height).
- **Zero Retouching:** Red-eye reduction or skin-smoothing filters are forbidden.
- **Eyeglasses:** Zero tolerance for glasses.
